# LinkedIn draft (Offset Security page)

**Quantum-safe before it's urgent.**

Today we're releasing CipherHorizon, the open-source tool behind our post-quantum readiness work.

NIST IR 8547 sets the dates: quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms are deprecated after 2030 and disallowed
after 2035. Traffic recorded today with a classical key exchange can be decrypted once a large quantum computer
exists. The first step of any migration is knowing where you stand.

CipherHorizon gives every TLS endpoint you run a readiness level from L0 to L4, with a TLS scanner that shows its
work: every protocol, suite and check, each with its source. It inventories the machines behind those endpoints,
merges everything into one CycloneDX CBOM, and ranks systems for migration by how long their data must stay secret.

It's self-hosted, sends nothing anywhere, and is free under Apache 2.0.
